This mod is a reimagining of another mod of a similar name, tweaking Minecraft equipment progression for casual players to actually make sense within the games progression system, by adding diamond ingots, the new way to craft diamond equipment, and tweaking properties of different armors and weapons.

What do I mean by this? Well, gold is now an upgrade from iron, while still being less durable like vanilla, and netherite still is an upgrade from diamond, just being a bit slower.

To still make netherite a choice for the completionist, I added a new enchantment called 'Swift Strike' which increases your weapons attack speed! You can find it as a treasure enchantment, or find a gold sword with the enchantment already applied, for those who want a really broken sword as a reward for exploring, or those who have a disenchanting mod and still want to be able to salvage the enchantment for their end-game gear.
